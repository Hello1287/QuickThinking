Welcome to Quick Thinking Math Competition!
Quick Thinking Math Competition is a buzzer-style competition that requires students to answer problems as fast as possible.
This reflects the style of competition found in many prestigious elementary and middle school math competitions!
All competitions are double-elimination tournament style. Most matches are Best of 5. The championship matches are Best of 7. Tournaments usually take less than 30 minutes.
(If you do not know what double-elimination tournament style is, click Rules/FAQ to find out)
Competitions will be held weekly, with problems that are both challenging and solvable.
IMPORTANT: At the moment, Quick Thinking is available for students in 1st to 6th grade. While this website will work on mobile devices, it will look better on a computer.
